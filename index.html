Lease

Flexibility, with a plan.

Whether you’re finding a place to live or leasing a property you own, make the timing, terms, and numbers work for what comes next.

Talk about leasing
Get the timing right.Coordinate availability, notice periods, applications and move-in.
Know the real terms.Rent is only part of the commitment. Understand fees, restrictions and renewal terms.
Keep the next move open.Make today’s lease work with a future purchase, sale or relocation.

Invest

Opportunity meets discipline.

Talk about investing
Define the objective.Cash flow, appreciation, development, short-term use or long-term hold.
Underwrite the reality.Acquisition, financing, taxes, carrying costs, rent, vacancy and exit.
Stay disciplined.A compelling property isn’t automatically a compelling investment.
